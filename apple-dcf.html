Apple Inc. DCF Valuation

An integrated financial model of Apple (AAPL) that links the income statement, cash flow statement and balance sheet, and values the company with two terminal value methods: perpetuity growth and exit multiple. It includes Best / Base / Worst scenarios and WACC sensitivity tables.

Takeaway: under these assumptions the model values Apple below the $340 reference price. Across the full sensitivity grids the perpetuity method ranges from $136.73 to $335.26 per share and the multiple method from $255.14 to $333.09, so no cell reaches $340. Only the best-case scenario under the multiple method ($366.81) is above it. The terminal value makes up 79% of enterprise value in the perpetuity method and 83% in the multiple method, so the result is highly sensitive to the discount rate and terminal assumptions.

ASSUMPTIONS

Key assumptions

  • Forecast: 2026F to 2030F, valuation date 30 June 2026 with a partial-period adjustment for the first year.
  • Revenue: grows from $416.2 bn (2025A) to $714.6 bn (2030F), about 11% a year, driven mainly by Services ($109.2 bn to $283.1 bn).
  • Margins: operating margin rises from 32.0% (2025A) to 37.0% (2030F).
  • Terminal year: a steady state where every segment grows at the terminal growth rate and margins stay at 2030F levels.
  • Working capital: receivable days 29, inventory days 9, payable days 100.
  • Capital: net cash of $33.8 bn added to enterprise value; 15,116 million diluted shares.
  • Reference price: $340.00.

MODEL

Structure

  • Income statement by product line, with a Services build
  • Cash flow statement and balance sheet (balance check = 0 in every period)
  • Working capital, depreciation and PP&E schedules
  • Unlevered free cash flow
  • DCF with perpetuity and exit multiple methods
  • Dashboards with a scenario switch and sensitivity tables
